How To Apply

Throughout the admissions process, we will get to know each other and assess how we can best support your child to ensure success at BCHS. For more information on applying see below:

Complete our online application form by the deadline. You can find the application portal here. Please gather all required documents (transcripts, recommendations, etc.) before starting.

If you have any questions about the application process, email us at admissions@baycityhigh.org.

At Bay City High, written placement tests help place students in the right academic level. Applicants take assessments in core subjects like math, English, and world languages, either on campus or virtually. These tests are not pass/fail but used to support course placement decisions alongside transcripts and interviews.

For more details on study resources or formats, download our Placement Test Guide .

After review of your application and test results, we will invite you and a parent/guardian for an interview. No special preparation is needed.

Interviews typically occur between October and February. We will coordinate scheduling via email.

Offers of acceptance will be communicated through our Admissions Portal by the specified date. You’ll receive instructions on how to accept your offer and submit your deposit.

If you have further questions after the decision, please contact admissions@baycityhigh.org.

Thank you for taking the time to learn more about our school. It is our privilege to share with you all the exciting opportunities that are unique to our students’ high school careers. You will discover how we prepare students to excel academically in their post-secondary studies and beyond

A visit to Bay City High is a personalized and welcoming experience designed to help prospective families truly get to know our school community. Campus tours are available by appointment throughout the school year and begin with a warm greeting from our Admissions Team. Visitors are then guided by a student ambassador—often a current student leader—who offers an authentic perspective on daily life at Bay City High.

The tour includes stops at key areas such as classrooms, labs, arts studios, athletic facilities, the dining hall, and our Innovation Commons. Families also have the opportunity to meet with teachers, observe classes in session, and speak one-on-one with administrators. Tours typically last 60 - 90 minutes and can be tailored based on specific interests, such as STEM, arts, athletics, or student leadership.

We also offer optional shadow days, allowing prospective students to spend part of the day paired with a host student to experience the flow of classes, lunch, and school culture firsthand. All visits are booked through our Admissions Office, and we welcome your questions every step of the way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Bay City High is a private day school serving students in grades 9 through 12.

Grade 9 students at Bay City High typically take seven core and elective classes designed to provide a strong foundation for high school success. These include:

  • English 9 - Literature, composition, and analytical writing
  • Algebra I or Geometry - Based on placement
  • Biology - Including lab-based investigations
  • World History & Cultures - A global perspective on early civilizations to modern times
  • World Language - Choice of Spanish, French, or Mandarin
  • STEAM Foundations - Interdisciplinary course blending science, tech, engineering, art, and math
  • Arts or Music Elective - Options include studio art, choir, band, or drama

Students may also choose to add a ninth period elective or support class, such as Study Skills, Debate, or Coding Lab.

At Bay City High, we believe every student learns differently, and we’re committed to meeting those unique needs through a robust range of supplemental academic support programs. Students can access services such as our Learning Strategies Program, which provides individualized coaching in time management and organization, and our Reading & Writing Center, where students receive personalized support on assignments and literacy skills.

Additionally, we offer a Math Lab, Homework Help Club, and a Study Skills Program designed to build confidence and academic independence. For students with documented learning differences, our Academic Accommodations team works closely with families and teachers to create tailored support plans. These programs ensure that all learners—whether they need extra support or additional challenges—can thrive and reach their full potential.
