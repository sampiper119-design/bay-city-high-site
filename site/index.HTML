Experiencing Bay City

Nestled in the heart of Bay City, New York, Bay City High sits on a beautiful landscape, minutes from the waterfront and the bustling downtown historic district. Our close-knit community enjoys small class sizes, that foster collaboration, personalized attention, and lifelong friendships. Students explore dozens of clubs and service-learning projects, develop their talents in award-winning arts and music ensembles, and compete in local and regional athletics. Proximity to top tech firms, museums, and cultural venues means internships, guest-artist workshops, and field-study opportunities are always within reach.
