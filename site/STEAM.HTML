The STEAM program at Bay City High is a dynamic, cross-disciplinary initiative that blends Science, Technology, Engineering, the Arts, and Mathematics to foster innovation, creativity, and real-world problem-solving.

Designed for curious and forward-thinking students, the program emphasizes hands-on learning through advanced coursework, collaborative projects, and access to state-of-the-art labs and makerspaces. From coding and robotics to digital design and environmental engineering, Bay City's STEAM curriculum encourages exploration across fields while building critical thinking and 21st-century skills.

Students graduate with a strong academic foundation, a portfolio of original work, and the confidence to lead in college, careers, and beyond.

Science

Science at Bay City High is hands-on, innovative, and central to the school's STEAM program. Students dive into biology, chemistry, physics, and environmental science through labs, projects, and real-world challenges.

With modern equipment and opportunities like the Coastal Science Fair and SharkTech Expo, students explore big ideas and build skills for future careers in science and technology.

Technology

Technology at Bay City High empowers students to become creators, not just consumers.Through coding, robotics, digital media, and app development, students engage in hands-on projects that bridge innovation and real-world impact.

With access to modern tools like 3D printers, VR labs, and collaborative maker spaces, tech is fully integrated into the school's STEAM program.

Engineering

Engineering at Bay City High challenges students to design, build, and innovate. Through hands-on projects in robotics, structural design, and sustainable tech, students apply math and science in creative ways. From bridge-building competitions to engineering challenges in the SharkTech Expo, Bay City's STEAM program fosters problem-solving, teamwork, and future-ready skills in every aspiring engineer.

Arts

At Bay City High, the arts bring creativity and expression to the heart of the STEAM program.

Students explore visual arts, digital design, music, theater, and multimedia as essential tools for innovation. Whether designing graphics for tech projects or composing original scores, the arts help students think imaginatively, communicate ideas, and connect disciplines–proving that creativity fuels discovery.

Mathematics

Mathematics at Bay City High is the foundation of its STEAM program, equipping students with the problem-solving and analytical skills needed across all disciplines. From algebra to data science and applied math in engineering and tech, students learn to think critically, model real-world scenarios, and tackle complex challenges with confidence and precision.

Bay City High is home to state-of-the-art STEAM facilities, including dedicated science labs, a robotics and engineering workshop, digital media studios, and a makerspace equipped with 3D printers and laser cutters. These collaborative spaces are designed to support hands-on exploration, innovation, and interdisciplinary learning across all areas of the STEAM curriculum.

1 / 10
The Biosphere
A cutting-edge biology and environmental science lab where students explore the complexities of life and ecosystems through hands-on research and real-world applications. Outfitted with climate-controlled terrariums, microscopes, water testing stations, and digital data sensors, the lab supports everything from cellular biology to sustainability studies. Whether analyzing marine samples from the nearby bay or conducting experiments on renewable resources, students use The Biosphere as a living classroom to investigate, innovate, and protect the natural world.
2 / 10
The SharkLab
A dynamic chemistry lab where curiosity ignites and formulas come to life. Equipped with modern fume hoods, digital spectrometers, and safe lab stations, the space is designed for rigorous experimentation in everything from reaction kinetics to environmental chemistry. Named in honor of the school's fierce mascot, The SharkLab fuels student discovery through hands-on investigations and project-based learning, turning young scientists into confident problem-solvers and innovators.
3 / 10
The Force Field
an interactive physics lab where theory meets experimentation. Outfitted with motion sensors, electromagnetism kits, optics stations, and digital data loggers, the lab empowers students to explore the fundamental laws of nature through real-world applications. Whether analyzing projectile motion or testing circuits, students gain hands-on experience in a high-tech environment designed to foster critical thinking, precision, and innovation–all within a space energized by the school's forward-thinking STEAM spirit.
4 / 10
The MechTank
The innovation hub for robotics and engineering enthusiasts. Equipped with VEX and LEGO robotics kits, 3D printers, CNC machines, and soldering stations, the lab empowers students to design, build, and program real-world solutions from the ground up. Whether developing autonomous bots or prototyping mechanical systems, students collaborate in a space built for creativity, precision, and problem-solving–all while embracing the hands-on spirit of Bay City High's STEAM program.
5 / 10
The Cave
A cutting-edge computer science and fabrication lab where digital ideas come to life. Outfitted with high-performance workstations, 3D printers, laser cutters, and microcontroller kits, The Cave is the creative den for coders, designers, and makers alike. Students explore everything from app development and game design to physical computing and rapid prototyping–bridging virtual logic with tangible innovation. In this immersive tech environment, curiosity leads the way, and every project carves out new potential.
6 / 10
The Creators' Lab
A dynamic digital media studio where storytelling meets technology. Equipped with professional-grade cameras, editing suites, green screens, and audio production tools, the space empowers students to craft films, animations, podcasts, and visual content. Whether producing news segments, music videos, or digital art, students gain hands-on experience in media production and creative communication. The Creators' Lab is where ideas are captured, edited, and brought vividly to life.
7 / 10
The Wave Room
A cutting-edge film and animation studio designed for aspiring directors, animators, and storytellers. Outfitted with industry-standard software, soundproof editing bays, motion capture capabilities, and a dedicated screening area, it's the creative hub where cinematic visions come to life. From stop- motion shorts to digital animation and live-action films, students use The Wave Room to explore narrative, technique, and visual impact in a studio that rides the crest of innovation.
8 / 10
The EchoCave
A modern music tech lab and rehearsal space where creativity meets sound science. Outfitted with digital audio workstations, MIDI controllers, recording booths, and acoustic instruments, the EchoCave supports everything from beat-making and composition to ensemble practice. Whether students are mixing tracks, scoring films, or perfecting performances, the space amplifies talent and fosters collaboration across genres and skill levels.
9 / 10
The Color Room
A vibrant, light-filled studio where imagination takes form. Designed for both traditional and digital art, the space features easels, drafting tables, touchscreen tablets, and a wide array of materials for painting, drawing, sculpture, and mixed media. As the creative heart of the STEAM program, The Color Room encourages exploration, self-expression, and visual storytelling in every brushstroke and pixel.
10 / 10
The Launchpad
The central hub for innovation, exploration, and creation within the STEAM program. This state-of-the-art facility brings together cutting-edge labs, collaborative studios, and high-tech classrooms under one roof, fueling student discovery across science, technology, engineering, the arts, and mathematics. With flexible learning spaces, industry-grade equipment, and a future-forward design, The Launchpad is where ideas ignite, projects take flight, and students launch into the possibilities of tomorrow.

STEAM Program Projects

Nestled just beyond Bay City High's science wing, the Bay City Biodome is a state-of-the-art, climate-controlled ecosystem where students explore biology, environmental science, and sustainability in a living classroom. From hydroponic towers and native plant gardens to simulated rainforest and desert biomes, the dome supports hands-on research and year-round cultivation projects.

Whether studying pollinator behavior, testing soil pH, or developing eco-solutions through the school's GreenTech initiative, students use the Biodome as both a lab and a launchpad for environmental innovation. It's not just a greenhouse–it's a living lab for the next generation of climate-conscious thinkers.

Bay City High's flagship outreach initiative that brings science, technology, engineering, and math beyond the classroom and into the heart of the community. Through mobile maker carts, public science demonstrations, neighborhood coding pop-ups, and student-led workshops, the program empowers learners to apply their skills in real- world settings.

Whether repairing community bikes with engineering students, teaching elementary schoolers how to code, or creating data-driven art installations in local parks, STEM-to-Street turns learning into impact–and Bay City into a living lab.

Bay City High's annual celebration of innovation, creativity, and interdisciplinary learning. Students present their most ambitious projects across science, technology, engineering, arts, and math–from AI-powered apps and sustainable architecture models to kinetic sculptures and multimedia storytelling.

Held in The Launchpad, the showcase draws peers, parents, industry partners, and community members for a dynamic exhibition that reflects the school's commitment to future- forward education. It's not just a presentation–it's a platform for ideas to shine, connect, and inspire.

Bay City High's hands-on innovation hub where engineering meets creativity. This student-run makerspace empowers aspiring inventors, coders, and builders to design, prototype, and refine real-world solutions–from robotics and electric vehicles to smart tech and mechanical systems. Outfitted with fabrication tools, 3D printers, laser cutters, and collaborative workstations, SharkWorks fosters a culture of experimentation, teamwork, and fearless problem-solving.

Bay City High's signature entrepreneurial pitch event, where STEAM students showcase bold, innovative ideas to a panel of faculty, local business leaders, and alumni mentors. Inspired by real-world startup competitions, teams present products, prototypes, or social impact solutions–competing for funding, mentorship, and the chance to bring their concepts to life.

Blending creativity, business savvy, and technical skill, SharkTank@BayCity turns classroom learning into high-stakes, real- world opportunity.

An immersive, interdisciplinary space where students explore the science, engineering, and logistics behind crisis response. Using virtual reality, physical modeling, and data analytics, students simulate natural and manmade disasters–like hurricanes, earthquakes, or chemical spills–and design strategic response plans.

From environmental science to civil engineering, the lab challenges students to think critically, collaborate across disciplines, and prepare for real-world challenges where innovation and preparedness can save lives.

The STEAM for Social Impact initiative empowers students to apply science, technology, engineering, arts, and math to address real-world issues in their communities. Whether designing accessible tech, building sustainable solutions, or creating awareness through digital storytelling, students use innovation to drive equity, empathy, and change.

With a focus on collaboration and civic responsibility, the program bridges classroom learning with meaningful action–proving that STEAM isn't just about invention, but about impact.

Bay City High has built a strong reputation for excellence in regional and national STEAM competitions. From robotics to digital art, students consistently place at the top of their fields. With standout performances in contests like the FIRST Robotics Competition, Scholastic Art & Writing Awards, Envirothon, and Science Olympiad, the school has earned a legacy of innovation, creativity, and teamwork.

Each year, Bay City High teams bring home medals, showcase projects at national events, and secure scholarships–cementing the school's place as a STEAM powerhouse.
