Featured Work:

Window Seat

by Jada M., Grade 11

*Note: This poem is an original creative asset produced explicitly for this student portfolio demonstration. The student character name is entirely fictional.*

In the back row, by the window’s light,
I sketch dreams in the margins of math,
Watching birds write stories on the sky,
Wishing I could follow their path.

The teacher’s voice, a steady stream,
Flows beneath my drifting gaze—
But I’m chasing thoughts too wide for desks,
In winds that whisper brighter days.

Between these halls and city blocks,
I carry hope like hidden ink—
That one day soon, I’ll step beyond
And be the poet I dare to think.
