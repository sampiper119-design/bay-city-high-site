At Bay City High, student life is vibrant, diverse, and deeply community-driven. As a private day school located in the heart of Bay City, New York, students thrive in a supportive environment that blends academic excellence with rich extracurricular engagement. Whether competing on the field, performing on stage, exploring innovation in The Launchpad, or leading student-led initiatives, learners are encouraged to find their voice and pursue their passions. With over 50 clubs and year-round events Bay City High cultivates not just scholars—but confident, compassionate citizens ready to make an impact.

  • Student Council President: Leads the student council, represents the entire student body, oversees major school events, and works directly with administration to advocate for student needs.
  • Student Council Vice President: Supports the president, steps in when the president is unavailable, and often oversees specific projects or committees.

At Bay City High, advisors and mentors are the heart of student support—offering personalized guidance that goes beyond the classroom. As a private day school nestled in the heart of Bay City, Bay City High offers a flexible, reliable transportation system tailored to meet the needs of its diverse student body. With a fleet of eco-friendly buses serving key neighborhoods and boroughs—including uptown, midtown, and surrounding suburbs—students enjoy a safe and efficient daily commute. Bike racks, designated carpool zones, and partnerships with local transit services encourage sustainable travel. The school's Transportation Office also coordinates late buses for after-school programs, ensuring accessibility to academic, athletic, and extracurricular opportunities.


Bay City High embraces a business casual dress code that reflects both professionalism and individuality. Students are required to wear the school colors—classic light and dark blue, along with pink—while accent colors such as white, black, grey, and khaki are also permitted. Approved attire includes blazers, button-ups, polos, collared shirts, and plain t-shirts (when paired with a blazer), as well as sweaters, sweater-vests, plaid skirts, dresses, and overalls—all of which must be knee-length. BCHS Dress Code Do's and Don'ts

  • DO Wear Bay City colors: Classic light blue, dark blue, and pink as primary colors.
  • DO Wear blazers or cardigans over shirts, especially with plain tees.
  • DO Choose business casual tops: Collared shirts, polos, button-ups, sweater-vests.
  • DO Stick to knee-length bottoms: Skirts, dresses, and overalls must be at least knee-length.
  • DO Tuck in shirts when appropriate for a neater appearance.
  • DO Wear clean, polished shoes: Loafers, flats, boots, or dress sneakers in neutral tones.
  • DO Accessorize subtly: Simple jewelry, headbands, or scarves in uniform-compliant colors.
  • DO Keep outerwear appropriate: School-branded or neutral-colored jackets and coats.
  • DO Maintain good grooming: Neat hairstyles and clean, well-maintained clothing.
  • DON'T Wear neon, glitter, or overly bright patterns.
  • DON'T Wear athletic wear: Hoodies, joggers, gym shorts, or leggings (unless under a skirt/dress).
  • DON'T Wear distressed or ripped clothing
  • DON'T Wear graphic tees, logos, or large brand names (unless school-approved).
  • DON'T Wear overly tight or revealing clothing, including cropped tops or short skirts.
  • DON'T Wear hats indoors, unless for religious or medical reasons.
  • DON'T Don't wear flip-flops, slides, or slippers—even on warm days.
  • DON'T Don't forget your blazer: When wearing a plain t-shirt—this is required to elevate the look.
  • DON'T Don't use clothing as a platform for political or offensive messages.
