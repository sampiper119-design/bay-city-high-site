At Bay City High, the World Languages program is designed to cultivate global awareness, cultural appreciation, and communication fluency. Students can choose from Spanish, French, Mandarin Chinese, and Latin, with pathways from beginner through Advanced Placement and Honors levels. Interactive classes emphasize speaking, listening, reading, and writing, while integrating cultural studies through music, cuisine, film, and global current events. For advanced learners, immersive experiences such as language exchange programs, travel opportunities, and global literature electives enrich their linguistic and cultural competency. Our goal is to empower students to connect meaningfully with diverse communities at home and abroad.
