Welcome To Bay City High

Bay City High School stands as a cornerstone of education and community spirit in the lively, coastal town of Bay City, New York.

With an enrollment of approximately 500+ students, the school embraces a rich tapestry of cultures, backgrounds, and talents.

Known for its forward-thinking curriculum and strong commitment to student success, Bay City High offers rigorous academic programs including AP and honors courses, dual-enrollment partnerships with nearby colleges, and a comprehensive STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics) initiative. The school prides itself on creating a nurturing environment that encourages exploration, innovation, and leadership.

Students can immerse themselves in a variety of extracurriculars–from championship-winning athletic teams and nationally recognized performing arts groups to dozens of student-led clubs, such as Robotics, Model UN, Environmental Action, and LGBTQ+ Alliance. Bay City High is also a hub for community engagement.

Through internship opportunities, volunteer initiatives, and annual events like Career Exploration Day and the Bay City Cultural Festival, students gain hands-on experience and develop real-world skills that prepare them for college, careers, and civic life.

With a focus on inclusion, creativity, and academic excellence, Bay City High empowers every student to discover their passion and achieve their fullest potential–both in and beyond the classroom.

100 Years of Learning

Bay City High School, established in 1936, is a cornerstone of education in Bay City, New York. Constructed during the Great Depression, the school was part of a federal initiative to provide jobs and improve public infrastructure. Its design reflects the architectural trends of the era, emphasizing functionality and durability. Over the decades, Bay City High has evolved to meet the changing needs of its student population, incorporating modern educational programs and facilities. Today, it continues to serve as a vital institution, fostering academic excellence and community engagement.

1924

School Founded

Bay City High opens its doors with just 30 students and a bold vision for coastal education. Founded by educator Marlene H. Brooks, the school aimed to blend academics with environmental stewardship. As its first principal, Brooks championed hands-on learning tied to the bay’s unique ecosystem.

1950

Science Wing Added

Inauguration of our first dedicated laboratory, sparking decades of scientific discovery. Led by science teacher Dr. Alan Rivera, the lab was built with community support and student involvement. It quickly became a hub for innovative coastal and marine research projects.

1975

Performing Arts Center

Opening of the Bay City Auditorium, home to theatre, music, and community gatherings. Designed by local architect Nina Kwan, it was brought to life through a city-wide fundraising campaign. Drama teacher Elise Martin staged the first production, setting the tone for decades of vibrant performances.

2000

STEAM Initiative Launched

Integration of Arts into our STEM curriculum, forging the modern STEAM pathway. Spearheaded by Curriculum Director Jaime Torres, the initiative began with collaborative workshops between science teachers and local artists. Through grants from the Bay City Arts Council, students now engage in hands-on projects that blend engineering principles with visual and performing arts.

2024

100-Year Celebration

Organized by the Alumni Association and led by Gala Chairperson Linda Chen, the event came together through months of volunteer coordination and sponsorship drives. With donations from local businesses and a committee of students and faculty, the gala featured archival exhibits, performances by alumni musicians, and honored distinguished graduates.

Welcome From The Principal

Miss Emily Mayers has proudly served as the principal of Bay City High since 2018. Known for her visionary leadership and compassionate spirit, Miss Mayers is deeply committed to fostering a school culture rooted in inclusion, innovation, and integrity. A graduate of Columbia University’s Teachers College, she began her career as an English teacher and quickly rose through the ranks due to her dedication to student achievement and community outreach. Under her guidance, Bay City High has introduced award-winning STEM and arts integration programs, expanded mental health resources, and launched the "Voices of Bay City" initiative–a student-led podcast celebrating diverse perspectives. Miss Mayers is often seen cheering on the Wildcats at home games or leading morning assemblies with her signature energy and optimism. She believes every student has a story worth telling and a future worth building–and she’s here to help make that happen.

"Dear Bay City Families, Students, and Community Members,
Welcome to Bay City High–a place where curiosity is celebrated, diversity is embraced, and every student is empowered to rise to their fullest potential. It is an absolute honor to serve as the principal of a school so rich in spirit, talent, and promise. Each day, I’m inspired by the dedication of our teachers, the resilience of our students, and the support of families who believe in the power of education to transform lives.

Whether it's through hands-on STEM projects, spirited performances on our stage, or community-driven service initiatives, our students are constantly growing into compassionate, courageous leaders.

Here at Bay City High, we don’t just prepare students for graduation–we prepare them for life. We champion critical thinking, creativity, and kindness in all that we do. And as we continue to grow, we do so together–as one Bay City family.Thank you for being part of this incredible journey."

With pride and gratitude,
Miss Emily Mayers
Principal, Bay City High

At Bay City High, we are dedicated to creating a dynamic, inclusive, and forward-thinking educational environment where students are inspired to achieve their full potential. Our mission is to foster a culture of curiosity, creativity, and resilience that prepares each learner not only for academic excellence, but also for responsible global citizenship and a life of purpose.

We believe in the power of education to transform lives and empower individuals to become leaders, innovators, and compassionate contributors to society. Through a rigorous and balanced curriculum, enriched by real-world experiences, the arts, athletics, and service learning, we challenge our students to think deeply, act ethically, and value diverse perspectives.

Guided by a commitment to student voice, holistic growth, and continuous improvement, Bay City High is a place where every student is known, supported, and celebrated. We equip our learners with the skills, confidence, and character needed to navigate a complex world and make meaningful contributions wherever life takes them.

Our Core Values

Excellence

We strive for the highest standards in academics, leadership, and personal growth.

Integrity

We act with honesty, accountability, and respect in all that we do.

Inclusivity

We celebrate diversity and foster a community where every voice is heard and valued.

Curiousity

We encourage a love of learning, critical thinking, and a fearless pursuit of knowledge.

Compassion

We lead with empathy, kindness, and a commitment to service and social responsibility.

Collabs

We believe in the power of teamwork, strong relationships, and shared goals.

Resilience

We support perseverance through challenges, learning from failure, and growing stronger together.
