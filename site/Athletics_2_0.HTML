Facilities

Bay City High boasts a state-of-the-art athletic complex designed to support a wide range of varsity, junior varsity, and co-ed sports programs. The campus offers top-tier training, competition, and recovery amenities for student-athletes year-round.

The Shark Complex - Athletics Center

Fully equipped weight room, cardio machines, athletic training room, physical therapy suites, and hydration stations. All athletes have access to personalized training programs.

Main Stadium - The Shark Tank

A 4,000-seat multi-purpose stadium with turf field, LED scoreboard, and track. Home to football, lacrosse, and track & field events. Includes locker rooms, media booths, and night lighting for evening games.

The Cove - Diamond Complex

A Full-size turf infield with bullpens, batting cages, and dugouts. Adjacent practice diamond with clay infield and digital pitch clock, and regulation-size with shaded bleachers and covered dugouts.

Crest Fields - Multi-Sport Turf

A cutting-edge, all-weather playing surface designed to host a range of athletic competitions. Surrounded by bleachers and equipped with LED lighting and a digital scoreboard, built for speed, endurance, and high performance.

Tide Run Fields - Track Field

Eight-lane synthetic track with separate long jump/triple jump pits, pole vault runway, discus and shot put cages. Includes timing technology and digital replay capabilities.

The Reef - Table Tennis Arena

Climate-controlled training center with 12 competition-grade tables, seating gallery, spin analysis tools, and slow-motion video review for coaching.

The Shark's Lair - Basketball Court

Contains seating for over 1,000 fans, a polished maple court, and a full lighting and sound system. The court features a custom-painted shark-themed center logo, modern digital scoreboards, and retractable bleachers. Upstairs, a glass-fronted viewing gallery and media platform provide excellent sightlines. Practice hoops, locker rooms, and a team film room are located just off the court.

The Ice Den - Rink

A professional-grade indoor hockey facility with a full NHL-sized rink, elevated student section known as the "Shiver Zone," and climate-controlled spectator seating. The facility includes LED lighting, dual locker rooms, a skate sharpening room, and off-ice training space. A shark mural sprawls across the far wall, reminding all who enter: this is Shark territory.

Partnerships

Riverside Cricket Oval
A full grass wicket with professional pitch layout, boundary netting, and shaded viewing pavilion.

Harborview Golf Course
Short-game facility with putting greens, chipping area, and full swing stalls equipped with launch monitors. Bay City also partners with nearby Harbor Links Golf Club for full 18-hole rounds.

The Athletics Pavilian
In the heart of Bay City, the Athletics Pavilion is a state-of-the-art gym for gymnastics and cheer. It features Olympic-grade equipment, foam pits, stunt zones, and a climate-controlled arena built for peak performance.
