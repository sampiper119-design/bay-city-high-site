At Bay City High, academic support is an essential part of our student-centered approach. We understand that every learner is different, which is why we offer a wide range of programs to meet diverse academic needs. From peer tutoring and faculty-led help sessions to specialized options like the Study Skills Seminar and Learning Strategies Program, students receive tailored guidance that plays to their strengths.

For those needing extra reinforcement, Individualized Support Plans and Subject-Specific Clinics provide structured help, while enrichment opportunities ensure advanced learners are continually challenged. Whether building confidence or pushing for excellence, every student is supported with the tools and encouragement to thrive.

Level 1 Support
  • Two-on-one support with a Learning Specialist.
  • Regularly scheduled meetings during the academic day.
  • Individualized learning plan: strengths, opportunities, reflection.
  • Open-door support during flex time, free periods, and evenings.
Level 2 Support
  • Small group support (fewer than 4 students).
  • Regularly scheduled meetings during the academic day.
  • Individualized learning plan focusing on each student.
  • Open-door support during flex time, free periods, and evenings.
Level 3 Support
  • Weekly one-on-one meeting with a Learning Specialist.
  • Individualized learning plan: ongoing reflection and growth.
  • Open-door support in flex time, free periods, and evenings.

Credit Recovery Program

This program is designed to support students who need to retake a course to meet graduation requirements. Offered through a blend of after-school sessions, summer intensives, and online learning options, the program allows students to recover credits in core academic areas—such as Math, English, Science, and Social Studies—without falling behind their peers.

Each course is guided by a certified teacher who provides targeted instruction, progress monitoring, and personalized support to ensure student success.

Summer Bridge Programs

A supportive, two-week experience designed to help rising 9th graders transition smoothly into high school life. The program includes interactive workshops in math, english, and organization, as well as team-building activities and campus tours.

Students meet future teachers and classmates, get familiar with expectations, and develop strategies for success—all in a welcoming, low-pressure environment.
