At Bay City High, the Social Studies curriculum is designed to cultivate critical thinkers, informed citizens, and compassionate leaders. Through a diverse array of history, civics, economics, and cultural studies courses, students explore the complexities of human society—past and present—while sharpening analytical and communication skills. With an emphasis on inquiry-based learning, global awareness, and civic engagement, our program empowers students to connect historical knowledge to contemporary issues and their own roles in shaping the future.
