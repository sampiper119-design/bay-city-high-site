Bay City High boasts a vibrant and accomplished alumni network made up of innovators, creatives, scholars, and leaders. Graduates have gone on to attend top-tier universities, launch startups, lead community initiatives, and work at renowned organizations across industries. Whether pursuing medicine, engineering, the arts, or public service, our alumni continue to reflect the values of curiosity, purpose, and impact instilled during their time here. Many return as guest speakers, mentors, and partners— keeping the Bay City spirit alive across generations.

Staying Connected

Bay City High alumni stay connected through a variety of opportunities—from reunions and alumni mixers to career panels and mentorship programs. Graduates are invited to join the Bay City Alumni Network, receive quarterly newsletters, and participate in school events such as STEAM showcases and community service days. Many also serve as internship sponsors, guest speakers, or advisory board members, helping to inspire the next generation of students.

Get Involved!

Alumni and parent donations have played a pivotal role in shaping the continued success of Bay City High. Thanks to their generous contributions, the school has been able to expand its cutting-edge STEAM facilities, fund need-based scholarships, launch student-led initiatives, and support faculty innovation. These donations have directly impacted student experiences—from upgrading labs in The Launchpad to creating spaces like The EchoCave and The Creators' Lab. Additionally, contributions have helped sustain community outreach programs and supported the school's growing endowment.
